## 1. What is 5serving FarmMarket?

5serving FarmMarket is a mobile-first marketplace that connects farmers directly with buyers — no middlemen, fair prices, fresh produce.

**Three types of users:**

| Role | Who they are |
|---|---|
| **Buyer** | Anyone who wants to buy fresh produce directly from farmers |
| **Farmer** | A registered farmer who lists and sells their produce |
| **Admin** | The 5serving team who manages the platform |

---

## 2. How to Access the App

Open your browser and go to:
**https://5serving-farmmarket.vercel.app**

The app works on any mobile or desktop browser. You can also **install it** on your phone like an app:
- On Android: tap the menu (⋮) → "Add to Home Screen"
- On iPhone: tap Share (□↑) → "Add to Home Screen"

---

## 3. Signing In (OTP Login)

All users sign in using their **mobile number** — no password needed.

### Steps:
1. Open the app → tap your role (**Buyer**, **Farmer**, or **Admin**)
2. Enter your **10-digit mobile number**
3. Tap **Send OTP**
4. Enter the **6-digit OTP** you receive (via SMS, or shown on screen in demo mode)
5. Tap **Verify OTP** → you're in!

> **OTP expires in 10 minutes.** If it expires, tap "Resend OTP" (available after 30 seconds).

## 4. Buyer Guide

### 4.1 Browsing Produce

After signing in as a Buyer, you land on the **Market** tab (🏪).

- Scroll to browse all available produce from farmers
- Use the **search bar** to find specific items (e.g. "tomatoes", "mangoes")
- Use **category filters** (Vegetables, Fruits, Grains, etc.) to narrow results
- Tap the **Organic** filter to show only organic produce
- Tap any product card to see full details

### 4.2 Product Detail

Tap a produce card to open the detail screen:

- See full description, price, quantity available, harvest date
- Tap **✨ Get Tips** for AI-powered nutrition and cooking tips
- Tap **Add to Cart** to add to your basket
- Use **+** / **−** to adjust quantity

### 4.3 Cart

Tap the **Cart tab (🛒)** to review your basket.

- Change quantities using **+** / **−**
- Remove items by reducing quantity to zero, or tap the remove icon
- See your **total amount** at the bottom
- Tap **Proceed to Checkout** when ready

### 4.4 Checkout

Fill in your delivery details:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Full Name | Your name |
| Mobile Number | Pre-filled from your login |
| Delivery Address | Your full address including flat/house no, street, city, PIN |
| Community (optional) | Select your housing society or colony if listed |
| Notes (optional) | Any special instructions for the farmer |

**Choose a payment method:**

| Option | How it works |
|---|---|
| 💳 Pay Online (Razorpay) | UPI, Card, or NetBanking — instant confirmation |
| 📱 Pay via UPI | Scan QR or pay to 5serving@upi, then share screenshot |
| 💵 Cash on Delivery | Pay when your produce arrives at your door |

Tap **Place Order** to confirm.

### 4.5 Order Confirmation

After placing an order you'll see the **Order Confirmed** screen with:
- Order number (e.g. #5SF1234567890)
- Items and total
- "What happens next?" steps
- A **Track My Order** button showing the delivery timeline

### 4.6 Viewing Your Orders

Tap the **My Orders tab (📦)** at the bottom to see all your orders.

- Tap any order to expand it and see full details
- The **progress tracker** shows where your order is:

```
📋 Placed → ✅ Confirmed → 🚚 Dispatched → 📦 Delivered
```

### 4.7 Cancelling an Order

You can cancel an order **only if it is still Placed or Confirmed** (before the farmer dispatches it).

1. Go to **My Orders (📦)**
2. Tap the order you want to cancel
3. Tap **✕ Cancel Order**
4. Confirm by tapping **Yes, cancel order**

> Once an order is **Dispatched or Delivered**, it cannot be cancelled through the app. Contact support in that case.

## 5. Farmer Guide

### 5.1 Registering as a Farmer

If you are a new farmer:
1. Open the app → tap **Farmer**
2. Enter your mobile number → receive OTP → verify
3. Since you are new, you'll be taken to the **Registration Form**
4. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Village / Town
   - District and State
   - Mobile Number
   - Crop types you grow (tap to select)
5. Tap **Register Farmer**
6. You'll see a **"Registration Submitted"** screen

> Your registration needs to be **approved by the admin** before you can start listing produce. This usually takes up to 24 hours.

### 5.2 Logging In (Returning Farmer)

1. Open the app → tap **Farmer**
2. Enter your registered mobile number → OTP → verify
3. You're taken to your **My Farm** dashboard

> If your account is still pending approval, you'll see a "Pending Approval" message. Wait for admin to approve you.

### 5.3 My Farm Dashboard

Your home screen shows:
- **Your profile** — name, village, crops, number of listings
- **My Produce** — all items you have listed
- Tap any produce card to view or edit it

### 5.4 Adding New Produce

Tap the **Add Produce tab (➕)** or the **+ Add Produce** button.

Fill in:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Produce Name | Country Tomatoes |
| Category | Vegetables |
| Unit | kg |
| Quantity available | 50 |
| Price per unit (₹) | 28 |
| Harvest Date | 2026-06-01 |
| Organic? | Yes / No |
| Description | Write your own, or tap **✨ AI Describe** |

Tap **✨ AI Describe** to auto-generate a warm marketplace description using the produce name, category, and your farm details.

Tap **Save Produce** when done. Your listing goes live immediately.

### 5.5 Managing Your Produce

From **My Farm**, tap any produce card to open the detail screen.

You can:
- **Toggle availability** — hide or show the item in the marketplace (useful when stock runs out)
- View full produce details

### 5.6 Managing Orders

Tap the **Orders tab (📦)** to see all orders placed for your produce.

**Dashboard stats at the top:**
- Total Orders
- Pending (placed + confirmed)
- Delivered
- Total Revenue (your items only)

**Each order shows:**
- Order number and date
- Buyer name
- Your items in the order
- Order status and payment status

**Tap any order** to expand and see:
- Buyer's phone number and delivery address
- Full item list with quantities and amounts
- An **action button** to advance the order

### 5.7 Advancing Order Status

As a farmer, you manage the order through these steps:

| Current Status | Your Action | Button |
|---|---|---|
| Placed | Confirm you have the stock | ✅ Confirm Order |
| Confirmed | Pack and dispatch | 🚚 Mark as Dispatched |
| Dispatched | Delivered to buyer | 📦 Mark as Delivered |

Tap the button in the expanded order view to advance to the next step.

### 5.8 Downloading Order Report

Tap the **⬇ CSV** button (top right of Orders screen) to download a full spreadsheet of your orders. Useful for your records.

## 6. Admin Guide

### 6.1 Signing In as Admin

1. Open the app → tap **Admin**
2. Enter your registered admin mobile number
3. Verify OTP → you're in the Admin Panel

> Only phone numbers configured in **ADMIN_PHONES** can access the admin panel.

### 6.2 Admin Panel Overview

The admin panel has **7 tabs**:

| Tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Overview | Stats dashboard + recent orders |
| Approvals | Review pending farmer registrations |
| Farmers | Manage all approved farmers |
| Buyers | View and manage all registered buyers |
| Produce | View and manage all listed produce |
| Orders | View all orders, update status, cancel orders |
| Communities | Manage community groups |

### 6.3 Overview Tab

Shows at a glance:
- Number of approved farmers, pending approvals, total produce listings, total orders, registered buyers, and total revenue
- A banner alert if there are pending farmer approvals (tap to go straight to Approvals)
- 5 most recent orders

### 6.4 Approvals Tab

Shows all farmers who have registered but are **waiting for approval**.

For each pending farmer, you can see:
- Name, village, district, state
- Phone number
- Crops they grow
- Registration date

**Approve:** Tap **✓ Approve** — the farmer can now log in and list produce.
**Reject:** Tap **✕ Reject** — the farmer is notified and cannot list produce.

### 6.5 Farmers Tab

Lists all approved farmers.

- Tap the 🗑 (delete) icon to remove a farmer
- An inline confirmation ("Are you sure?") appears — tap **Yes** to confirm or **No** to cancel
- Tap **+ Register Farmer** to add a new farmer directly

### 6.6 Buyers Tab

Lists all buyers who have placed at least one order.

Shows each buyer's name, phone, delivery address, community, and order count.

- Tap 🗑 to remove a buyer profile
- Confirm before deleting

### 6.7 Produce Tab

Lists all produce currently in the system.

For each item:
- **Toggle switch** (right side) — turn availability on/off without deleting the listing
- **🗑 Delete** — permanently removes the listing (with confirmation)

### 6.8 Orders Tab

Lists all orders across all farmers and buyers.

Tap any order to expand it and see:
- Buyer name, phone, address
- All items and amounts

**Available actions:**
- **→ confirmed / → dispatched / → delivered** — advance the order status on behalf of the farmer
- **✕ Cancel** — cancel the order (shows confirmation first)
- **⬇ Export All Orders (CSV)** — download a full spreadsheet of all orders

### 6.9 Communities Tab

Manage community groups (e.g. apartment complexes, office colonies, housing societies).

**Add a community:**
1. Tap **+ Add Community**
2. Fill in: Community Name, Location, Type, Estimated Members
3. Tap **Save Community**

**Delete a community:** Tap 🗑 next to the community name, then confirm.

Communities appear as options in the buyer checkout form.

## 7. FAQ

**Q: I didn't receive my OTP via SMS. What do I do?**
The app may be in Demo Mode — look for a yellow banner on the OTP screen that shows your code directly. Enter those 6 digits.

**Q: My farmer registration was rejected. What now?**
Contact the 5serving admin team. They can re-register you or let you know what information was missing.

**Q: I placed an order but want to change the delivery address.**
Contact the farmer directly (their number is shown in the order) before the order is dispatched. Address changes are not available in-app after checkout.

**Q: Can I order from multiple farmers in one order?**
Yes. Add items from different farmers to your cart and checkout once. Each farmer will see only their items in their Orders screen.

**Q: The produce I wanted shows as "unavailable". Why?**
The farmer may have marked it as hidden (out of stock or not in season). Check back later or contact the farmer.

**Q: How do I know my payment went through?**
After checkout you'll see a green "Order Confirmed" screen. For online payments (Razorpay), payment status shows as "paid". For COD, it shows "pending" until delivery.

**Q: I'm a farmer. Can I edit my produce after listing?**
You can toggle availability on/off from the My Farm screen. To change price or quantity, tap the produce card.

**Q: As admin, how do I export orders for a specific farmer?**
Go to **Orders tab → ⬇ Export All Orders (CSV)**. The CSV includes farmer names for each item so you can filter by farmer in Excel or Sheets.
